You are never alone 🌷

It is never your fault. The person who hurts or scares you is the one doing wrong.
Trust your feelings. If something feels wrong or uncomfortable, it is okay to say NO and walk away.
Good touch / Bad touch. Nobody has the right to touch you in a way that makes you uncomfortable. Tell a trusted adult.
Stay online-safe. Don't share private photos or OTPs. Block, report and screenshot anything harmful.
Travel smart. Walk in groups, stay in lit and busy places, share your live location with someone you trust.
Secrets that scare you should never be kept. Brave girls ask for help. 💜
